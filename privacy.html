Privacy Policy

What leaves your phone, what doesn't, and how to switch the rest off.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

The short version. Your costs, loads and reports stay on your device, and in your own iCloud account if you turn iCloud Sync on — we never receive them. The app sends anonymous usage statistics so we can see which features matter and catch crashes; you can switch that off in Settings → Privacy. There are no ads, no trackers, and no account to create.

1. Introduction

Essential Dev Team ("we", "our", "us") operates TruckProfit (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and how to control it. By downloading or using TruckProfit you agree to it.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide

The calculator data you enter — operating costs, load details, ZIP codes, mileage, truck names and financial figures — is stored on your device and, if iCloud Sync is on, in your own iCloud account (see section 4). We never receive, transmit to our servers, or store your calculator inputs.

2.2 Subscription information

If you buy a TruckProfit Pro subscription, payment is processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect, process or store payment information such as card numbers, billing addresses or bank details. Apple handles all billing, renewals and cancellations.

2.3 Anonymous usage statistics

To improve the App, TruckProfit sends anonymous usage information: a random installation identifier that is not linked to your identity or Apple ID; your device model and iOS version; the app version; your device's time zone, region and language settings; accessibility settings in use (such as larger text); when sessions start and end and the last screen viewed; which features you use (for example "a load was saved", with the number of stops, the driver-pay type, whether the load was profitable and whether dates were set; "a report was exported" with its period; a distance estimate; a backup export or restore; a truck added); paywall views and taps; purchase, trial and restore events (whether you have an active subscription or trial and the plan you chose — never prices paid, receipts or transaction IDs); App Store review prompts; and crash and performance diagnostics provided by Apple (MetricKit).

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, postal address, location, IP address (our servers do not store or use it), advertising identifier (IDFA), vendor identifier (IDFV), or the contents of anything you enter in the App — such as rates, costs, profit, ZIP codes or truck names.

  • Not linked to you, not used for tracking: the installation identifier is random, exists only in this app, and is deleted if you delete the app. We never combine it with other apps' or companies' data.
  • Not shared or sold: we do not share this data with third parties and do not use it for advertising.
  • Where it is stored: on a database operated for us by our hosting provider, Supabase, accessible only to us.
  • How long: usage records are deleted automatically after 13 months, crash diagnostics after 6 months. Only aggregated totals (such as "sessions per day") are kept longer, and they contain no installation identifiers.
  • Turn it off: Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data. When it is off, nothing is collected or sent, and statistics not yet sent are deleted from your device.
  • Delete what was sent: copy your Installation ID from Settings → Privacy and email it to us; we will delete all records for it.

2.4 Distance estimates and maps

To place your stops on the map and calculate driving routes (Pro), the ZIP codes you enter are sent to Apple's mapping service. That request goes to Apple, not to us, and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

2.5 Weather alerts (optional, Pro)

If Weather Alerts on Route is on, points along the calculated route (map coordinates only) are sent to the US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) to look up official alerts. No personal information is included, and nothing is sent to us. Turn it off in Settings → Appearance → Weather Alerts on Route.

2.6 Your location (optional)

If you tap "Show my distance to pickup" and allow location access, the App uses your device's approximate location to show the empty miles from your truck to the pickup. Your location is used only on your device and in the directions request to Apple; it is never sent to us, stored, or included in usage statistics. You can turn it off with "I'm dispatching this load", or remove access any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

3. How we use information

  • Improving App performance, stability and features
  • Diagnosing and resolving technical issues
  • Understanding which features are used, to guide development priorities

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing to third parties, or profiling.

4. Data storage and security

Your calculator data, saved loads, cost settings and history are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage, protected by your device's built-in security — passcode and encryption. If you enable the App lock, authentication uses Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode through Apple's local authentication system; biometric data never leaves your device and is never available to the App or to us.

iCloud Sync: when iCloud Sync is on (Settings → Backup & Sync), the same data is also stored in your own iCloud account so it appears on your other devices. It is protected by Apple and we cannot access it. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time.

Backups you export: the App can export a backup file of your settings and load history. That file goes wherever you send it — Files, iCloud Drive, email, another app — and we never receive it.

We do not store your calculator or financial data on any server of ours. The only data that leaves your device for us is the anonymous usage statistics described in section 2.3 (if left on), sent over an encrypted connection. Deleting the App permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.

5. Third-party services

  • Apple StoreKit — in-app subscription management and payment processing
  • Apple iCloud — syncs your data between your own devices when iCloud Sync is on
  • Apple Maps — places ZIP codes on the map and calculates routes, including the optional drive from your location to the pickup
  • Apple App Analytics — anonymised, aggregate usage statistics provided by Apple
  • US National Weather Service — official weather alerts along a route, when Weather Alerts on Route is on
  • Supabase — hosts our anonymous usage statistics on our behalf (see section 2.3). It does not use the data for its own purposes.

Apple's services are governed by Apple's own privacy policies; we encourage you to review Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent or share your personal information with third parties. Period. We would disclose information only if required by law, such as in response to a valid court order or legal process.

7. No accounts, no ads

TruckProfit has no user accounts and no sign-up. It shows no advertising, contains no advertising SDK, and does not track you across apps or websites.

8. Children's privacy

TruckProfit is a business tool intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Because your data lives on your device (and your own iCloud), you control it at all times. You can:

  • Turn off usage statistics — Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data
  • Delete usage statistics already sent — email us your Installation ID (Settings → Privacy)
  • Delete your data — remove individual entries in the App, or uninstall it entirely
  • Export your data — using backup export, or PDF export (Pro)
  • Manage subscriptions — through your Apple ID settings

Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; email us and we will honour them.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
