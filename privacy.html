Privacy Policy

How we handle your data — spoiler: we respect it.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Essential Dev Team ("we," "our," or "us") operates TruckProfit (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

By downloading or using TruckProfit, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

TruckProfit is designed with privacy in mind. The calculator data you enter — including your operating costs, load details, ZIP codes, mileage, truck names and financial figures — is stored on your device and, if iCloud Sync is on, in your own iCloud account (see section 4). We never receive, transmit to our servers, or store your calculator inputs.

2.2 Subscription Information

If you purchase a TruckProfit Pro subscription, your payment is processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not collect, process, or store any payment information such as credit card numbers, billing addresses, or bank account details. Apple handles all subscription billing, renewals, and cancellations.

2.3 Anonymous Usage Statistics

To improve the App, TruckProfit sends anonymous usage information: a random installation identifier that is not linked to your identity or Apple ID, your device model and iOS version, the app version, your device's time zone, region and language settings, accessibility settings in use (such as larger text), when sessions start and end and the last screen viewed, which features you use (for example "a load was saved", with the number of stops, the driver-pay type, whether the load was profitable or not and whether dates were set; "a report was exported" with its period; a distance estimate, a backup export or restore, a truck added), paywall views and taps, purchase, trial and restore events (whether you have an active subscription or trial and the plan you chose — never prices paid, receipts or transaction IDs), App Store review prompts, and crash and performance diagnostics provided by Apple (MetricKit).

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, postal address, location, IP address (our servers do not store or use it), advertising identifier (IDFA), vendor identifier (IDFV), or the contents of anything you enter in the App — such as rates, costs, profit, ZIP codes or truck names.

  • Not linked to you, not used for tracking: the installation identifier is random, exists only in this app, and is deleted if you delete the app. We never combine it with other apps' or companies' data.
  • Not shared or sold: we do not share this data with third parties and do not use it for advertising.
  • Where it is stored: on a database operated for us by our hosting provider, Supabase, accessible only to us.
  • How long: usage records are deleted automatically after 13 months, crash diagnostics after 6 months. Only aggregated totals (such as "sessions per day") are kept longer, and they contain no installation identifiers.
  • Turn it off: Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data. When it is off, nothing is collected or sent, and statistics not yet sent are deleted from your device.
  • Delete what was sent: copy your Installation ID from Settings → Privacy and email it to us; we will delete all records for it.

2.4 Distance Estimates and Maps

To place your stops on the map and calculate driving routes (Pro), the ZIP codes you enter are sent to Apple's mapping service. This request goes to Apple, not to us, and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

2.5 Weather Alerts (optional, Pro)

If Weather Alerts on Route is on, points along the calculated route (map coordinates only) are sent to the US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) to look up official weather alerts. No personal information is included, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn this off in Settings → Appearance → Weather Alerts on Route.

2.6 Your Location (optional)

If you tap "Show my distance to pickup" and allow location access, the App uses your device's approximate location to show the empty miles from your truck to the pickup. Your location is used only on your device and in the directions request to Apple; it is never sent to us, stored, or included in usage statistics. You can turn it off with "I'm dispatching this load", or remove access any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

3. How We Use Information

Any information we collect is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Improving App performance, stability, and features
  • Diagnosing and resolving technical issues
  • Understanding which features are used, to guide development priorities

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing to third parties, or profiling.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your calculator data, saved loads, cost settings, and history are stored locally on your device using iOS standard storage mechanisms. This data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including device passcode and encryption.

iCloud Sync: when iCloud Sync is on (Settings → Backup & Sync), the same data is also stored in your own iCloud account so it appears on your other devices. It is protected by Apple and we cannot access it. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time.

We do not store your calculator or financial data on any server of ours. The only data that leaves your device for us is the anonymous usage statistics described in section 2.3 (if left on), sent over an encrypted connection. If you delete the App, all locally stored data is permanently removed from your device.

5. Third-Party Services

TruckProfit may use the following third-party services:

  • Apple StoreKit — for in-app subscription management and payment processing
  • Apple iCloud — to sync your data between your own devices when iCloud Sync is on
  • Apple Maps — to place ZIP codes on the map and calculate routes, including the optional drive from your location to the pickup
  • Apple App Analytics — for anonymized, aggregate usage statistics
  • US National Weather Service — official weather alerts along a route, when Weather Alerts on Route is on
  • Supabase — hosts our anonymous usage statistics on our behalf (see section 2.3). It does not use the data for its own purposes.

Apple's services are governed by Apple's own privacy policies. We encourage you to review Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles your data.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties. Period.

We may disclose information only if required by law, such as in response to a valid court order or legal process.

7. Children's Privacy

TruckProfit is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Your Rights

Since your data is stored on your device (and your own iCloud), you have full control over it at all times. You can:

  • Turn off usage statistics — Settings → Privacy → Share Usage Data
  • Delete usage statistics already sent — email us your Installation ID (Settings → Privacy)
  • Delete your data — by deleting individual entries within the App or by uninstalling the App entirely
  • Export your data — using the PDF export feature (Pro)
  • Manage subscriptions — through your Apple ID settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
