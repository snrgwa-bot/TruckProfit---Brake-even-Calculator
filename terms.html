2. What TruckProfit is

TruckProfit is a calculator for truck drivers, owner-operators and small fleets. It estimates a break-even cost per mile from the operating costs you enter, evaluates the profitability of individual loads, keeps a history of those loads, and produces reports from them. Some features are free; others require a TruckProfit Pro subscription.
